# Mark drafts kept for later

Drafts from the search for a cleaner Ares mark (26–27 Nov 2026), kept in case they are wanted later. The chosen one
is the lion coin in `brand/ares-coin.svg` (black on off-white) and `brand/ares-coin-dark.svg` (off-white on black).
All files are 512 × 512 SVGs with the background included; colours as in `EMPIRE.md` → The crown language.

| Files | What it is |
|---|---|
| `coin-lion-*` | The chosen coin (head with the lion on the shoulder plate) in gold on black, and gold with a red crest. |
| `coin-lion-reference.png` | The black-and-white drawing the lion coin was traced from (Codex image generation, prompt in `brand/LOGO_PROMPT.md` → The coin mark). Trace it again for a cleaner or larger version. |
| `coin-struck-*` | Struck coin with a medium crest and a few bold cut lines; the most readable coin at 40 px. |
| `coin-long-crest-*` | A long crest that follows the edge of the circle; best from 128 px up. |
| `coin-engraved-*` | Fine engraving: helmet scroll and a shoulder guard of strips, like Roman cuirassed busts; best from 256 px up. |
| `spear-*` | The upright spear: a kite-shaped blade with a socket ring and a short shaft. The clearest mark at 16 px. |

Colour endings: `gold` gold on black · `gold-red` gold with crest red · `gold-red-ring` gold with a red socket ring ·
`white-on-black` off-white on black · `black-on-white` black on off-white · `black-on-stone` black on a slightly darker
paper tone, so the circle shows on white pages.
