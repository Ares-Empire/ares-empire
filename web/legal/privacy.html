2. Hosting and server log files

The websites, the account server and the server services of the apps run on a server operated by netcup GmbH, Karlsruhe, in Germany. Each time a website is accessed and each time an app sends a request, the following data is processed:

  • the IP address of the requesting device,
  • the date and time of the request,
  • the address requested (URL) and the status code of the response,
  • for the account server also the browser identification (user agent) and the previously visited page (referrer).

This data is stored in log files. The request log of the account server is deleted after 5 days, the server's other log files after 14 days. The demo pages show made-up sample data and store nothing about their visitors.

The purposes are providing the websites and the services, the security of the systems, error analysis and the prevention of misuse, for example by limiting login attempts. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest lies in these purposes.

3. Cookies

After login on the website, a cookie (empire_session) containing the login token is set. It is technically necessary to stay logged in, is valid for 30 days and is deleted on logout. The legal basis is § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. No other cookies and no analytics or tracking services are used.

When a light or dark appearance is chosen on aresium.de or on the contact pages of www.ares-empire.de, the choice is kept in the browser's local storage (localStorage) so that the next visit opens in it. It is not transmitted and can be deleted with the browser's site data. The legal basis is § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG.

4. Contact forms and email

When one of the contact forms on www.ares-empire.de (among them www.ares-empire.de/contact) or on aresium.de (aresium.de/contact) is used, the email address, the message, the name (if provided), the chosen topic (for example a request for an app), the language of the page and the time of the message are processed. The message is stored on the account server and forwarded to the controller by email; the email address provided is used as the reply address and, for a request for an app, to send the link for installing it. The IP address is used to limit the number of messages per sender (section 2). When the controller is contacted by email, the data sent is processed in the same way.

The purpose is handling and answering the enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; where the enquiry concerns a user account or the use of the services, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Messages are deleted 12 months after the enquiry has been closed.

5. User account

A user account is required for access to the Ares Empire services; Aresium can be used without one (section 6). For a user account, the following data is processed:

  • email address and password (only a hash of the password is stored),
  • a name, if provided,
  • the status of the account (email address confirmed, account approved), the services enabled and requested and the language for emails (German or English),
  • the times of creation and modification.

An account can be created on the website or in any of the iPhone apps. After sign-up, a confirmation link is sent to the email address provided. Every account and every request for access to a further service is reviewed and approved by the controller, who receives a notification containing the email address, the requested services and the language of the account for this purpose. Further emails are sent after approval and for resetting the password.

The purpose is providing access to the services and managing access rights. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Notices of logins from new devices

To protect the account, the account server stores, for each of the last five devices or browsers used to log in, a hash (MD5) of the IP address and the browser identification (user agent), together with the times of first and last use; the IP address itself is not stored. When a login comes from a new device, an email stating the time, IP address and browser of that login is sent to the account's email address. The hashes are deleted when the password is changed and together with the account. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest lies in protection against unauthorised access.

Storage period and deletion

Account data is stored until the account is deleted; accounts that have not been approved are stored until they are approved or rejected. The account can be deleted at any time in any of the iPhone apps (Settings → Account); its deletion can also be requested by email to {{OWNER_EMAIL}}. The account and all associated data are then deleted in every service. Access to a single app can be ended as well; that app's data is then deleted and the account remains.

6. iPhone apps

Login

The apps use the user account described in section 5. On login, the email address and password are sent to the account server. The login token is stored in the iPhone's keychain, shared by the Ares Empire apps on that device, and deleted on logout. This storage is strictly necessary for using the apps (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG); the legal basis for the processing is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Contact and support

Messages sent with "Contact & support" are stored on the account server together with the type of message, the text, the app and its version, the iOS version and device type, the language and the user account, and are forwarded to the controller by email; the account's email address is used as the reply address. Purpose and legal basis are as in section 4. Messages are deleted 12 months after the enquiry has been closed, at the latest together with the user account.

Push notifications

If notifications are allowed in iOS, the app sends the device token assigned by Apple, together with the user account, the app and its language, to the Ares Empire push service. Notifications are delivered through the Apple Push Notification service; Apple receives the device token and the content of the notification. For error analysis, the last 200 notifications sent (time, user account, title, text, link) are stored.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Notifications can be switched off at any time in the iPhone's settings. The device token is deleted on logout, when access is withdrawn, when Apple reports it as invalid, and at the latest 60 days after the app last registered it.

The apps contain no analytics, advertising or tracking functions.

Aresium

Aresium analyses account transactions that are exported as a file from the online banking of a bank or payment service and imported into the app. The files and the information they contain (account, booking and value date, amount, booking text, payee or payer and their account details, balance), the chosen categories, the assignments and the rules learned from them are processed and stored only on the iPhone. Transactions are assigned to categories on the iPhone, using a list of known shops included in the app and corrections made in the app. This data is not transmitted to the Ares Empire servers or to any third party; the controller has no access to it.

Receipts can be photographed with the camera or chosen from the photo library. The photo is read on the iPhone with the text recognition of iOS and then discarded; only the recognised text and the shop, date, amount, currency and payment method are stored, also only on the iPhone, where a receipt is linked to the matching transaction. This data is not transmitted to the Ares Empire servers or to any third party either.

The processing on the iPhone serves to provide Aresium's functions (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); storing the data on the iPhone is strictly necessary for this (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

The data is deleted with "Delete Aresium data on this iPhone" in the app's settings, with "Forgot the code?" while the app is locked, and when the app is removed from the iPhone. For the widgets, the current month's totals are kept in a storage area on the iPhone shared by the Ares apps and are deleted together with the other data; while the lock is on, no totals are kept there.

With "Export", the data can be saved or shared as a file and restored from such an export. Device backups, such as iCloud Backup, may include Aresium's data; the terms of the respective provider apply. Exported files and device backups are not deleted when the data is deleted in the app.

Aresium can be locked with Face ID and/or a six-digit code of its own. Face ID is checked by iOS on the iPhone; the app only receives the result. The own code is stored only as a hash in the iPhone's keychain.

If Aresium does not recognise the format of a bank file, the format can, after confirmation, be sent voluntarily together with the bank's name as entered, so that Aresium recognises it in future. Only the column headings and their meaning, the separator, the date and number format, the bank's name and the app's version and language are sent; no transactions, amounts, names or account numbers. A format that appears to contain such information is neither sent nor accepted. Likewise, the name of a bank that Aresium does not know yet can be sent voluntarily; only the name as entered and the app's version and language are sent. The information is stored on the account server and forwarded to the controller by email. The IP address is processed as described in section 2 and is also used to limit the number of submissions. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest lies in supporting further banks and bank formats and in protection against misuse. The information is deleted once the bank or format has been added to Aresium, at the latest after 12 months.

Messages sent with "Contact & support" in Aresium are processed like messages sent with the contact form (section 4); the type of message, the app version and the iOS version are sent as well.

Resend processes data in the USA. Resend is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). Apple transfers data from the EU to the USA on the basis of standard contractual clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

9. Storage period

Personal data is deleted as soon as it is no longer needed for its purpose and no statutory retention obligations apply. The periods for the individual processing operations are stated in sections 2 to 6.

To restore the services after errors or outages, their data is backed up daily. Backups are kept for 7 days on the server and, encrypted, for at most 6 months on a computer of the controller. Deleted data is removed from the backups at the latest when these periods end. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interest is the security and availability of the services.
