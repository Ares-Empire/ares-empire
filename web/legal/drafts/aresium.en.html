Aresium

Aresium requires the user account described in section 5. Import files, payments, categories and sorting rules are processed and stored on your iPhone. The controller cannot access these financial data stored on your iPhone. Unless you use the automatic sorting described below, these data are not sent to Ares Empire's servers or to Groq.

The data remain on the iPhone when you log out and become accessible again when you log in with the same account. You can delete or export the local data in Aresium. A device backup you use may also include Aresium's data; deleting data in the app does not delete backups or exported files.

Aresium: optional automatic sorting

Automatic sorting is switched off by default. If you switch it on, payments that Aresium could not categorise on your iPhone are sent to the Aresium server at netcup in Germany and from there to the GroqCloud AI service (Groq) to select a suitable category. For each payment, the date, amount, booking text, counterparty and a random reference are sent, together with the names of your categories. The reference replaces the bank's identifier but does not make the other details anonymous. Your login token is used on the Aresium server to control access and limit requests per account; it is not forwarded to Groq.

Groq processes these details, including in the USA, and returns a category from your list for each payment or no assignment. The result is stored on your iPhone and can be changed there. No payments are executed and no decisions about credit or access to other services are made.

The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Booking texts, counterparties and categories may also reveal special categories of personal data, such as information about health, religious beliefs or trade union membership. Where such information about you is processed, the legal basis is also your explicit consent under Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR.

The transmitted payment details, category names and responses are processed on the Aresium server only to answer the request; they are not stored in a database, logs or backups. Groq's Zero Data Retention is enabled for these requests; inputs and outputs are not retained there for reliability or abuse monitoring and are not used to train models. Groq collects technical usage metadata without the inputs and outputs. The technical server logs described in section 2 are separate and contain no payment content.

Automatic sorting is optional. You can use Aresium with local and manual sorting without it. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future by switching off automatic sorting in Aresium's settings. No further automatic sorting requests will then be sent. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it was withdrawn.
